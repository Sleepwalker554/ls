I am a Master student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Johns Hopkins University. I work as a Research Assistant at the [SMILE Lab](https://sites.google.com/view/jhusmile/homepage), affiliated with the [Center for Language and Speech Processing (CLSP)](https://www.clsp.jhu.edu/), under the supervision of Prof. [Berrak Sisman](https://engineering.jhu.edu/faculty/berrak-sisman/).

My research interests lie in speech and language processing and AI for health. I am interested in modeling acoustic and linguistic information to better understand and process human speech and language. My interests in large language models (LLMs) and large audio-language models (LALMs) focus on analyzing internal representations to understand model behavior and developing post-training methods to improve reasoning capabilities and overall performance.

I am also interested in applying AI models, including both small-scale and large-scale models, to the assessment of cognitive impairment and mental health, particularly the detection of Alzheimer's disease and depression. I aim to understand how acoustic and linguistic cues reflect health conditions and develop high-performing and reliable models across diverse real-world settings. My recent work has led to papers published in the AACL-IJCNLP 2026 Main Conference, Interspeech 2026, and IEEE SLT 2026, with further work submitted to ICASSP 2026 and ACL Rolling Review.

I received my bachelor's degree from Dalian University of Technology, where I worked with Prof. [Qiufen Xia](https://faculty.dlut.edu.cn/qfx/zh_CN/index.htm) on cold-start latency in serverless computing, resulting in a first-author paper and a granted patent.

Outside of research, I love [films and traveling]({{ base_path }}/life/). My dream is to travel to different places and attend film festivals around the world.
